The five that make it defensible

Every one of these is anchored to something real — a gazetted rate, a published standard, a licensed provider, a regulatory deadline. None of it needs inventing.

01 · THE RECORD

Vuka Record

Issue the work history as an Open Badges 3.0 credential. The spec reached Final Release on 29 June 2026 and is now a profile of the W3C Verifiable Credentials data model — so the badge is cryptographically tamper-evident and readable by any conforming wallet. The CV stops being a page on your server and becomes something she owns.

Open Badges 3.0 · W3C VC 2.09
02 · THE MONEY

Escrow & PayShap

Do not build escrow. Holding client funds makes you a third-party payment provider under SARB Directive 1 of 2007, and the Reserve Bank's activity-based authorisation framework — third draft out for comment to 15 June 2026 — adds mandatory segregation of client money. Integrate a licensed provider and pay out over PayShap in under ten seconds.

TradeSafe SP001191 · Truzo FSP 515395
03 · THE COST

Verification economics

Home Affairs' Online Verification System went to R10,00 per real-time check on 1 July 2025 — up from 15 cents — but off-peak batch verification is R1,00. Verify every new sign-up in an overnight batch at R1, and spend R10 only at the moment money is about to move. Same trust, a tenth of the cost.

R1,00 batch · R10,00 real-time10
04 · THE REACH

Data-free by right

Zero-rating job-seeking services is a condition of ICASA's 2022 spectrum licences binding MTN, Vodacom and Cell C, with a deadline of 15 January 2027. Vetting runs through the Social Innovation Register. Only about fifteen organisations have been zero-rated so far — the queue is short and the door is open now.

Deadline 15 Jan 202711
05 · THE BUYER

YES as the revenue line

Workers are free. The money is the employer who needs a Youth Employment Service placement: twelve months, at least R5 241 a month, worth up to two B-BBEE levels. Vuka's ladder is a pre-qualified, evidence-backed pipeline into exactly that — and the evidence pack is the part nobody wants to assemble by hand.

R5 241/month · up to 2 levels6
06 · THE GAP

Why now

SA Youth has 4,6 million work-seekers but is a matching network, not a credential. JOBJACK sells ranking to enterprise HQ. Ekasi to the Burbs launched this month and says outright it is "an open listing board, not a verification agency". Nobody in South Africa is issuing portable verified work credentials. That is the open lane.

No SA platform issues portable credentials12
